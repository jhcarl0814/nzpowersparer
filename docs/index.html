Power Sparer

We sell plug-in solar systems and offer on-site installation services to help you save on your electricity bills.

Details:

  • What:

    • A non-integrated solar generator:

      • Solar panel(s) (converting solar energy into DC);

      • (If multiple solar panels are connected in series) A bypass diode for each component (routing current preventing hotspots);

      • (If multiple solar panel lines are connected in parallel) A blocking diode (anti-reverse diode) for each line (preventing backflow);

      • A solar charge controller with maximum power point tracking (MPPT) (maximizing the output power of the solar panel(s) by adjusting the load);

      • Batter(y/ies) (for energy storage);

      • (If multiple batteries are connected in series) A battery balancer for each line (balancing the charge of each battery on the line);

      • An inverter (converting DC into AC);

      • An automatic transfer switch (used to switch between inverter power and mains power to power pluggable appliances).

    • Enough power strips and extension cords as needed to connect pluggable appliances throughout the home to the output of the transfer switch.

      • ⚠Only electricity consumed by pluggable appliances can be saved. We can't help with hard-wired appliances.

    • We will schedule an appointment with you to come to your home for installation.

  • Where:

    • It is recommended to place the generator (without solar panel(s)) in the central area of the house (usually the living room) to facilitate connection to pluggable appliances throughout the home. The device is non-intrusive and requires no modifications to your home.

      • No electrician is required for the installation.

      • ⚠Sufficient space should be reserved in the central area of the house (usually the living room).

    • ⚠A backyard is required, and sufficient space should be reserved in the backyard for placing solar panel(s).
